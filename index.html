    Services

    Daily prayers

    Five daily prayers in congregation and Jumu'ah every Friday.

    Learning

    Qur'an reading and tajweed, and Islamic studies for children and adults.

    New Muslims

    A friendly point of contact for anyone new to Islam, with no question too basic.

    Funerals and janazah

    Guidance and support for families at a difficult time.

    Youth and sisters

    Activities and gatherings for young people and for women.

    Community support

    Advice and signposting for neighbours who need a hand.

    About Da'watul Islam Havering North

    We are a community of Muslims in Havering North who come together to worship, learn and look after one another. Our doors are open to the wider neighbourhood too.

    Da'watul Islam Havering North is a project of Da'watul Islam UK & Eire (charity no. 1186155). This community shares information about Jumu'ah, Taraweeh, activities and news that affects the community.
