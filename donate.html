How to set up a standing order in your banking app

  1. Open your mobile banking app: Select "Pay & Transfer", choose "Set up a standing order" or "Regular payment".
  2. Enter our charity details: Sort Code 20-31-52, Account Number 23032523, and Account Name DAWATUL ISLAM UK & EIRE LTD.
  3. Choose your regular amount: Select monthly frequency (£30/month for operational costs or your chosen contribution), using your name and cause as reference.

Charity governance & FAQs

Complete financial integrity, 100% Zakah policy, and registered UK charity accountability.

  • 100% Zakah Policy

    We operate a strict 100% Zakah policy. Zero administrative costs or overheads are deducted from your Zakah contributions. Every single pound is distributed directly to verified, eligible recipients in need according to Islamic guidelines.

  • Registered UK Charity

    Da'watul Islam Havering North is a project of Da'watul Islam UK & Eire Ltd, a registered charity in England & Wales (Charity Commission Registration No. 1186155). All donations are handled in full compliance with UK charity law.

  • Can I set up £1 a day via Standing Order?

    Yes, absolutely. Setting up a monthly standing order of £30 is the most effective and sustainable way to support the daily running costs of our Farringdon Grove hub, ensuring our doors remain open for all five prayers 365 days a year.

  • How do I confirm my bank transfer?

    If you would like a receipt or confirmation for a bank transfer or pledge towards the Masjid Purchase Fund, simply drop a quick message with your transfer reference to our team via our WhatsApp community channel or at prayer times.
